ROBOTICS & MECHATRONICS

Blinker

Assistive Navigation Robot for Visually Impaired Individuals

01 / PROJECT OVERVIEW

Project Overview

Blinker is a dog-inspired mobile robotic system developed to assist visually impaired individuals with navigation. Designed to move along the ground, the robot integrates ultrasonic and infrared sensors, torque sensing, and a NI myRIO controller to support obstacle detection and navigation assistance.

02 / MECHANICAL DESIGN

Mechanical Design & Stair-Climbing Mechanism

The mechanical design of Blinker focused on developing a stable, lightweight mobile platform capable of navigating flat surfaces, climbing stairs, and descending them.

Six-Wheel Mobility System

Blinker features a six-wheel configuration supported by four mechanical linkages. The front linkages are independently articulated, allowing them to rotate freely around fixed axes when encountering stair steps. The rear linkages remain fixed and provide additional stability during climbing.
